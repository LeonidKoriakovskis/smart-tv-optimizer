How to Enable USB Debugging on your Xiaomi TV / Mi Box

One-Time Setup
1

Unlock Developer Options

On your TV, go to Settings > Device Preferences > About.
Scroll down to Build and press the remote's center OK button 7 times until you see "You are now a developer!"

2

Enable USB Debugging

Press Back, enter Developer Options (now visible in Device Preferences).
Scroll down and toggle USB Debugging to ON.

3

Find IP & Authorize

Check your TV IP under Settings > Network > Wi-Fi. Type it above and click Connect.
When your TV screen shows "Allow USB Debugging?", check "Always allow" and press OK.

Pre-Flight Checklist

  • ✓

  • ✓
    TV Remote in Hand: Keep your TV remote ready. You will need to press OK to authorize your computer when connecting for the first time.
Phase 2

One-Time TV Setup (Enable USB Debugging)

1
Open TV Settings: Click the gear icon on the top right of your TV home screen.
2
Open About & Build: Go to Device Preferences > About (or System > About).
3
Click 7 Times: Scroll down to Build and click the remote's center OK button 7 times until it says "You are now a developer!".
4
Turn on USB Debugging: Press Back, enter Developer Options, scroll down to USB Debugging and toggle it ON.
5
Get TV IP Address: Go to Settings > Network & Internet > click your Wi-Fi network and note down the IP (e.g. 192.168.1.150).

Frequently Asked Questions & Safety FAQ

Can this brick or damage my Xiaomi TV / Mi Box?

No. The tool uses Android's safe per-user service management (pm disable-user --user 0). The original firmware packages remain untouched in system storage. No bootloader unlocking or root is involved.

Will 4K Netflix, Disney+, and Prime Video continue working in 4K HDR?

Yes, 100%. Because the bootloader remains officially certified and locked, your Widevine L1 DRM security keys are fully preserved. Dolby Vision, HDR10, and 4K playback work seamlessly.

What if the "Allow USB Debugging" popup doesn't appear on my TV?

On your TV, go to Settings > Device Preferences > Developer Options. Turn USB Debugging OFF, wait 3 seconds, then turn it ON again. Return to Tab 1 on your PC and click Connect to TV.

How do I undo changes or restore a disabled app?

Simply navigate to the Restore Vault & Logs tab. Every disabled package is listed with a single-click "Restore" button that brings the app back to life instantly!
